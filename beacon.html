Nominate a Beacon

The Beacon Awards honor the Mississippi Gulf Coast businesses that guide our community forward. Nominations for the inaugural awards are open January – February.

Jan – FebNominations open
MarchFinalists + public voting
Late AprilAwards night

Categories

  • Beacon Business of the Year — excellence, integrity, community spirit
  • Brightest Newcomer — outstanding business under 3 years old
  • Guiding Light — exceptional customer service
  • Harbor Spirit — outstanding community giveback
  • People’s Choice — most community votes
  • Legacy Beacon — a Coast institution, 25+ years
  • Rising Tide — fastest-growing Coast business
  • Hometown Flavor — food & drink that tastes like the Coast
